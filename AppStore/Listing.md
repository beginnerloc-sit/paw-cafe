## Description
Open the cosiest café in town — one where the real stars have whiskers.

Paw Café is a relaxing idle game about running a little cat café on a sunny street. Customers wander in from the pavement, order kitty lattes and paw cookies, and fall in love with your cats. Earn coins, decorate, and grow your café from a tiny corner counter into a home for dozens of furry friends.

CATS, CATS, CATS
• 40 original cats to collect — tabbies, tuxedos, fluffballs and a few rare legends
• Every cat has its own personality: some nap in sunbeams, some steal cake, some never stop playing
• Befriend shy strays at the window with treats until they trust you enough to move in
• Use adoption tickets at the shelter for a surprise new friend

SNAP THE PERFECT SHOT
• Tap any cat to pull out your camera
• Customers ask for photos — "Pebble napping", "any cat playing" — catch the moment for big tips
• Fill your café photo wall and album, and post to the Whisker Feed to gain followers

BUILD YOUR DREAM CAFÉ
• Unlock a Window Nook, a Reading Room with a crackling fireplace, a sunny Terrace and a Cat Lounge
• Place tables, armchairs, cat trees, tunnels and beanbags anywhere you like
• Upgrade your barista, menu and tip jar to earn more

RELAX, IT'S IDLE
• Your café keeps running while you're away — come back to a full tip jar
• No timers to stress about, no ads, no in-app purchases
