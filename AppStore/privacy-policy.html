Paw Café: Cat Tycoon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Paw Café: Cat Tycoon ("the app") is made by Kenyon AI Innovation. We respect your privacy.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and the in-game photos you take are saved only on your own device, inside the app. They are removed when you delete the app. We never receive them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
